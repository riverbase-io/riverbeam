# Conventional 30-year fixed: guidelines

Conventional loans follow the conforming loan limit ($766,550 for a one-unit home in most counties).

## Credit
- Minimum credit score: 620.
- A 30-day late payment in the last 12 months needs a written explanation.

## Down payment and loan-to-value
- Minimum down payment: 3% for first-time buyers (97% loan-to-value), otherwise 5%.
- Below 20% down, private mortgage insurance (PMI) applies until the balance reaches 78% of the original value.

## Debt-to-income
- Maximum debt-to-income (DTI): 45%. DTI is total monthly debts, including the new housing payment, divided by gross monthly income.
- Income must be documented with a recent pay stub covering 30 days and two years of W-2s.

## Reserves
- Two months of housing payments in reserve are recommended above 90% loan-to-value.
