# Borrower FAQ

## What is debt-to-income (DTI)?
DTI is your total monthly debt payments, including the new mortgage payment, taxes and insurance, divided by your gross monthly income. A $1,800 housing payment plus $600 of other debts on $6,000 gross monthly income is a 40% DTI.

## What is loan-to-value (LTV)?
LTV is the loan amount divided by the home's price. A $400,000 home with $40,000 down has a 90% LTV.

## Which documents do I need?
A recent pay stub, your last two W-2s, two months of bank statements and a photo ID. Self-employed borrowers also need two years of tax returns.

## Is a rate quote a rate lock?
No. Quoted rates are indicative. A rate is locked only after an approved application and a signed lock agreement.

## How long does a pre-check take?
A loan officer reviews a submitted application within one business day. You will be told if more information is needed.

## Does checking my rate affect my credit?
The pre-check uses a soft credit pull, which does not affect your score.
