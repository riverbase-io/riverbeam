# Jumbo 30-year fixed: guidelines

Jumbo loans exceed the conforming loan limit and are kept by the lender.

## Credit and reserves
- Minimum credit score: 700.
- Reserves: twelve months of housing payments after closing.

## Down payment
- Minimum down payment: 20% (80% loan-to-value). No mortgage insurance.

## Debt-to-income
- Maximum DTI: 43%.
- Two years of tax returns are required in addition to pay stubs.
