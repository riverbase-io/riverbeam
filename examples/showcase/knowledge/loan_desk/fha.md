# FHA 30-year fixed: guidelines

FHA loans are insured by the Federal Housing Administration. The loan limit in standard-cost areas is $498,257.

## Credit
- Minimum credit score: 580 with 3.5% down; 500-579 needs 10% down and is not offered by this lender.
- Late payments in the last 12 months are reviewed by an underwriter; one isolated 30-day late is acceptable with an explanation.

## Down payment and loan-to-value
- Minimum down payment: 3.5% (96.5% loan-to-value). This lender requires 4%.
- Gift funds from family are allowed with a gift letter.

## Mortgage insurance
- Upfront mortgage insurance premium: 1.75% of the loan, usually financed.
- Annual premium: 0.55% for most loans, for the life of the loan below 10% down.

## Debt-to-income
- Maximum DTI: 50% with compensating factors (reserves, minimal payment shock); 43% otherwise.
