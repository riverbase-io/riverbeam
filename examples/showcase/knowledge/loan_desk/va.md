# VA 30-year fixed: guidelines

VA loans are guaranteed by the Department of Veterans Affairs for eligible veterans, service members and surviving spouses.

## Eligibility
- A Certificate of Eligibility (COE) is required; the lender can request it.
- Borrowers with full entitlement have no loan limit; this lender caps VA loans at $1,500,000.

## Down payment and fees
- No down payment (100% loan-to-value) and no monthly mortgage insurance.
- A one-time VA funding fee applies (2.15% for first use with no down payment); it is waived for veterans receiving disability compensation.

## Credit and income
- This lender's minimum credit score: 600.
- Maximum DTI: 41%, higher with residual income above the VA guideline.
