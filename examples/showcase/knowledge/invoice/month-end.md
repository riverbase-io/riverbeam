# Month-end close notes

## Cut-off

The books for a month close on the fourth business day of the next month. An invoice dated in the month is an expense of that month if the goods were received in it, even when the invoice is matched later. Goods received but not yet invoiced are accrued from the goods receipts.

## What AP does before the close

- Match every invoice received up to the last day of the month. An invoice left in "received" status at the close is listed for the controller.
- Clear or explain every invoice in "pending approval" or "on hold". The controller notes why each is still open.
- Check the goods receipts for the month against invoices: a receipt with no invoice after 30 days is raised with the vendor.
- Confirm the payments scheduled for the first week of the next month; they are not expenses of the closing month, but their invoices are.

## Reports for the controller

- Open invoices by status and age.
- Exceptions by vendor and by rule (price, quantity, duplicate), so repeat problems show up.
- Payments scheduled by pay date.
- Invoices rejected in the month, with the reason.

## After the close

A matched invoice that arrives after the close is booked in the open month and flagged as a prior-month item. The controller approves any change to a closed month.
