# Warranty and damaged items

## Warranty

- Electronics (headphones, speakers, grinders and other small appliances) carry a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date.
- Apparel and accessories are covered for manufacturing defects for 90 days.
- The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover wear and tear, drops, water damage or misuse.

## Damaged on arrival

When an item arrives damaged:

1. Ask the customer for the order number and a short description; a photo helps but is not required to open a ticket.
2. Open a ticket in category **damaged**.
3. A support agent offers a replacement or a full refund (see Returns and refunds). Items under $100 do not need to be sent back.

## Warranty claims

After the first 30 days, a defect is a warranty claim: a support agent arranges a repair or replacement. Refunds for warranty claims need a team lead (escalate the ticket).
