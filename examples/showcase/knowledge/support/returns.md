# Returns and refunds

## Returns

- Most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery**, unused and in the original packaging.
- Return shipping is free for wrong or damaged items. For other returns, a $6.50 label fee is deducted from the refund.
- Final-sale items (marked "final sale" on the product page) cannot be returned.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the refund is issued.
- A support agent issues every refund; the chat assistant cannot.
- **Damaged or defective on arrival:** full refund, or a free replacement, without returning the item when it is under $100. Ask for a photo.
- **Wrong item delivered:** we send the right item by express shipping at no cost, with a prepaid label for the wrong one. A refund is offered when the right item is out of stock.
- **Late or lost parcels:** see Shipping and delivery.
- A refund is never more than what the customer paid for the order.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund (up to 20% of the item price) can settle a minor cosmetic flaw when the customer prefers to keep the item.
