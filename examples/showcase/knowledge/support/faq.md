# Customer FAQ

**How do I track my order?** The tracking link is in the shipping email. The support assistant can also look it up with the order number.

**Can I cancel my order?** Yes, until it ships. After that, return it within 30 days.

**Do you price match?** No. We do adjust the price once if the same item drops in price within 14 days of purchase: ask support.

**How do I reach a person?** The assistant opens a ticket, and a support agent replies within one business day (Monday to Friday, 8 am–6 pm Eastern).

**Where is my refund?** Refunds reach the original payment method 5–7 business days after they are issued. The ticket shows the refund reference.

**Do you ship internationally?** Only to the United States and Canada.
