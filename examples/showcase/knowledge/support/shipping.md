# Shipping and delivery

Northwind Goods ships from Louisville, KY.

- **Standard shipping** is free on orders over $50 and takes 3–6 business days. Under $50 it costs $5.95.
- **Express shipping** costs $14.95 and takes 1–2 business days when ordered before 2 pm Eastern.
- Every order gets a tracking number by email when it leaves the warehouse. Carriers are UPS, USPS and FedEx.

## Delays

Carriers post weather and volume delays on the tracking page. When a parcel is late:

1. Up to 5 business days past the estimate, we ask the customer to wait: most late parcels arrive in that window.
2. After 5 business days past the estimate, or when tracking shows no movement for 7 days, the parcel counts as lost. We reship it at no cost or refund it in full; the customer chooses.
3. Express shipping fees are refunded when an express order arrives late, whatever the cause.

## Address changes

The address can change until the order ships. After that, the customer can redirect the parcel with the carrier.
