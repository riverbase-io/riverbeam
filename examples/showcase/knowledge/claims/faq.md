# Claims FAQ

## How do I report an accident?

Use the claims chat: it asks for the date, place, what happened, the other driver, the police report number and whether the car is drivable, then asks for a photo or a body shop estimate, and files the claim.

## How much do I pay?

You pay your deductible: **$500** for collision and **$250** for comprehensive on a standard policy. We pay the repair estimate minus the deductible, up to your limit.

## Do I need a police report?

For a collision with another driver, yes: give us the report number. Without one the adjuster takes a closer look before deciding. For a single-vehicle loss, a report is not required but helps.

## What if the car cannot be driven?

Say so when you report the loss. Rental reimbursement is **$40 per day for up to 10 days** while the car is repaired. Keep your receipts.

## How long does it take?

An adjuster usually reviews a filed claim within two business days. Payment normally arrives 3 to 5 business days after approval.

## Which repair shop do I use?

Any licensed shop. Get an estimate and send it with your claim. We pay the approved amount; we do not require a particular shop.

## What is not covered?

Racing, commercial or rideshare use, damage done on purpose, and wear and tear. See the policy wording for the full list.

## Will my claim raise my premium?

An at-fault claim can change your premium at renewal. A not-at-fault claim, or a comprehensive claim such as glass or hail, usually does not. Ask your agent for your own policy.

## What if my claim is denied?

You get the reason in writing. If you have a new estimate or documents, send them and ask for a second review.
