# Harbor Mutual auto policy wording

This is the standard wording for Harbor Mutual personal auto policies. Your declarations page lists the coverages, deductibles and limits that apply to you.

## Collision coverage

Collision pays to repair or replace your insured vehicle when it hits, or is hit by, another vehicle or an object, or when it rolls over. It applies whoever is at fault.

- **Deductible:** you pay the first part of the repair cost. The standard collision deductible is **$500**.
- **Limit:** the most we pay for one collision is the **collision limit** on your declarations page, or the actual cash value of the vehicle if that is less. A standard limit is **$25,000**.
- **How the payout is worked out:** the repair estimate minus the deductible, up to the limit.

## Comprehensive coverage

Comprehensive pays for loss to your vehicle that is not a collision: theft, vandalism, fire, flood, hail and other weather, falling objects, hitting an animal, and broken glass.

- **Deductible:** the standard comprehensive deductible is **$250**.
- **Limit:** the actual cash value of the vehicle, up to the comprehensive limit on your declarations page.

## Rental reimbursement

While your vehicle is being repaired after a covered loss, we reimburse a rental car up to **$40 per day for at most 10 days** (the amounts on your declarations page apply). Keep the rental receipts. Rental is not paid for a claim that is denied.

## What is not covered

We do not pay for loss caused by:

- **Racing:** racing, speed contests, or driving on a track or closed course.
- **Commercial or rideshare use:** carrying passengers or goods for pay, including rideshare and delivery work, unless you bought a commercial endorsement.
- **Intentional damage:** damage you or someone acting for you causes on purpose.
- **Wear and tear:** gradual deterioration, rust, mechanical breakdown and the cost of maintenance.

We also do not pay for personal belongings inside the vehicle (a homeowners or renters policy may), or for the loss of value after a repair.

## Your duties after a loss

Report the loss promptly, protect the vehicle from more damage, tell us the facts truthfully, give us a repair estimate or photos, and report a collision with another party to the police. We may decline a claim that is made with false statements.

## Policy period

Coverage applies to losses that happen between the start and end dates on your declarations page. A loss outside that period is not covered.
